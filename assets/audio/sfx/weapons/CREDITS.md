## Preparation for Castle Strike

Files were converted to mono 44,100 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV; leading silence was
trimmed to approximately 5 ms before the audible attack. Natural tails were
shortened as needed, with 2 ms entrance and 35 ms exit fades. Peaks were
normalized to 0.85. Arrow landings layer two credited wood recordings with a
12 ms offset and a final 25 ms fade. No synthesized oscillators were added.

`manifest.json` records each output's duration, format, source mapping, and
SHA-256 checksum. Source and license pages were checked September 4, 2026.
