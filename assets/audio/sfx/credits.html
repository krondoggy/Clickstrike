Castle Strike sound credits

Thanks to the performers and sound artists whose recordings bring the battlefield to life.

Human and creature death cries

Michel Baradari / Apollo Music — 11 male human pain/death sounds and 15 monster grunt/pain/death sounds, submitted by qubodup. Licensed under CC BY 3.0.

Selected recordings have been trimmed, normalized, and given short fades. Playback adds minor pitch variation and mixes them with other sounds. The original artists do not endorse this game.

Female scream

AuraVoice / Nocturnal_Vanguard — Female Scream 1. Licensed under CC0 1.0. The original recording is bundled unchanged and mixed during playback.

Swords, armor, and impact foley

Jan Schupke / Vehicle — Fantasy Sound Effects (Tinysized SFX). Recorded sword clashes, metal strikes, wood, apple cuts, and leather landings, licensed under CC0 1.0. Selected recordings are trimmed, normalized, converted to mono WAV, and layered for arrow landings.

Bow release and weapon swings

artisticdude, submitted by Ogrebane for Summoning Wars — Battle Sound Effects. Used under the offered CC0 1.0 license. Selected recordings are trimmed, normalized, and converted to mono WAV.

Additional interface, magical, and explosion layers are generated by Castle Strike. Detailed source-to-file mappings are included alongside the bundled recordings.
