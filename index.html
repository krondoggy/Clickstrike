How to Play

  • Mine Gold and Forage to fund your war. Buy Economy upgrades in Treasury — miners and foragers auto-click at 40% yield.
  • Your army eats food every second while fighting. Watch the net food rate in the top bar. Run out and troops weaken, healers stop, and units desert.
  • Wave 1 starts with a countdown. Then battle loops — destroy the enemy keep (right) before yours (left) falls.
  • Army panel: click a unit to train (faster than Auto). Toggle Auto on several types for a mixed draft. Counters matter — spears beat cavalry, archers beat infantry, and so on.
  • Heroes are expensive gold + food sinks. When they fall, pay 40% of hire cost to rez. One hero at a time.
  • Win a wave for gold and advance. Lose and you drop gold, food, and a wave. Progress autosaves in your browser.
