# Castle Strike portrait atlas

The complete 27-unit faction portrait set is documented in [faction-art-notes.md](faction-art-notes.md). This earlier twelve-cell atlas is retained alongside those three faction atlases.

`portraits.png` was generated using the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. It contains original fantasy character designs with a classic painted fantasy RTS aesthetic. No external game assets were imported.

- Image: 1448 × 1086 pixels, PNG, 3,143,530 bytes.
- Layout: four columns and three rows, twelve equal 362 × 362 square cells.
- Cells have no margins, gutters, frames, labels, or text.
- CSS atlas sizing: `background-size: 400% 300%`.
- Column background positions: `0%`, `33.333333%`, `66.666667%`, `100%`.
- Row background positions: `0%`, `50%`, `100%`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production-ready 4-column by 3-row character portrait atlas texture for a premium dark-fantasy RTS game UI, Castle Strike. Create ONE landscape 4:3 image, ideally 1536x1152 pixels, divided into exactly 12 EQUAL SQUARE portrait cells, four across and three down. Every portrait completely fills its own square edge-to-edge. NO MARGINS, NO GUTTERS, NO BORDERS, NO frames, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO decorative grid lines. Crisp exact divisions at 25%,50%,75% across and 33.333%,66.667% down. Painterly high-end classic Warcraft III cinematic fantasy RTS feel, but all ORIGINAL character designs, richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustrations, chunky heroic silhouettes, tactile weathered metal, leather and cloth, dramatic luminous rim lights and strong readable expressive faces. Each separate cell contains one centered head-and-shoulders bust tightly cropped, face clearly legible even as a tiny game icon. Each has its own dark black/forest shadow background. Consistent scale and close framing across all twelve. No shared panoramic scene. Row 1 left to right: (1) stern human steel footman wearing silver helmet and blue-and-gold heavy plate, cold blue rim light; (2) vigilant green-hooded female elven archer with pointed ears and auburn hair, leather shoulder guards, green forest rim light; (3) noble middle-aged human paladin with full silver beard, ornate silver and gold plate, radiant golden aura behind head; (4) wizened white-haired human battle mage wearing deep blue robes, blue fire and arcane light. Row 3 left to right: (9) skeletal death knight with icy teal eyes in dark blackened plate and torn violet hood; (10) pale hooded necromancer with gaunt face, deep forest-green hood, green magic illuminating face; (11) huge grotesque stitched abomination with sickly purple-grey flesh, crude stitches, heavy iron jaw, intimidating expressive face; (12) majestic dark blue frost dragon head with long icy ivory horns and sharp blue-white eyes, sapphire scales and frost breath. All heads must remain entirely within their respective square. Strong variation and no repeated character. Rich oil-painted concept art, atmospheric, polished game-ready detailed portraits, high contrast, no flat vector art, no photorealism, no UI.
