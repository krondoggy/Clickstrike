How to Play

  • Campaign levels: each victory unlocks the next level with a stronger AI. Defeat keeps you on the same level so you can retry. Progress is saved in the browser.
  • Gold trickles in all the time. Economy upgrades raise your gold per second. Buy and place the next wave while the field is still fighting.
  • Hire recruits units and Economy; Research upgrades your army. Class tracks buff every matching unit (Forged Swords for melee, Bodkin Arrows for ranged, Arcane Focus for magic/heal). Each unit also has specialties like Vitality (HP), Plating (armor), Reach (range), Haste (speed), Blast, or Siegecraft. Towers still get +HP/+ATK/+range. Living units update immediately.
  • Deploy: click a barracks unit, then a highlighted spawn tile. Click a placed unit to select it — empty tiles glow to move, occupied tiles swap. Click the barracks (or double-click the unit) to send it back. Esc clears the selection.
  • When the 30s timer hits zero, your roster deploys automatically. Combat never pauses — survivors stay and the next countdown starts immediately, until a keep falls.
  • Mobile: Shop to hire, then tap a glowing spawn tile on Battle to choose where that unit lives. Barracks under the field holds units you have not placed yet.
  • Inspect: hover shop, barracks, or field units for full stats and Strong/Weak counters. On a phone, press and hold — the card docks at the bottom. Research cards preview the next level in green.
  • Shortcuts: H = help · M = mute music · Esc = clear selection.
  • Win streak: holding more units on the field than the enemy raises your streak and bonus gold per second (shown in the top bar).
  • Roster cap starts at 2 and grows by 1 each wave (up to 24). Early fights stay small; save gold for better units or economy instead of flooding the field.
  • Towers guard each approach and fire at nearby enemies. Destroy them, then siege the keep.
  • The first keep to fall loses. Keeps are fortified (1500 HP) — chip damage is reduced; bring a real siege force (catapults help).
  • Champions (wave 4+): one hero per match — Bulwark, Bonesinger, or Raid Captain. Pay gold to rez if they fall during the countdown.
  • Abilities: Brace, Volley, Shield Bash, Charge, Sanctuary, Arcane splash, Guardian Aegis taunt.
  • New roles: Militia cheap soak · Assassin dives backline · Grenadier splash · Catapult siege.
